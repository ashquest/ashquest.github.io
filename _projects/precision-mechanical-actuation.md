At DG Takano, I worked on the design of a precision mechanical actuation system for variable fluid control. The project focused on improving function, manufacturability, and modular assembly.

### Contributions
- Engineered a 3-position mechanical actuation system for variable fluid control using CAD and rapid prototyping.
- Optimized the magnetic coupling interface, increasing retention force by 300%.
- Applied Design for Manufacturing (DFM) to reduce unit production cost and improve sustainability.

### Result
The work combined mechanical design rigor with practical manufacturing constraints, resulting in a more robust and cost-effective solution.
